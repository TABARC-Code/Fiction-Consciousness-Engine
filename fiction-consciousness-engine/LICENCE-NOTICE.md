## The specification

`references/01`, `02` and `03` are the maintainer's own specification documents, byte-identical to the upload. Section A of `04` and `05` is byte-identical too; section B holds operating notes, with additions logged in `references/lattice.md`, section 9.

## Adapted material

No wording below was copied. Each source was rewritten and restructured.

- **Expanded book-intelligence specification.** The maintainer's own extended specification for author voice reconstruction and manuscript development, supplied with this build. It is the basis for the nine subskills, the dossier, blueprint and continuity templates, auditor note B12, and Frame D. It grew out of a book-analysis skill posted publicly on Reddit (r/claudexplorers), whose separate frames for argument, fiction and history, and reader-verdict format, also shaped `references/07`. That post stated no licence.
- **Voice-extraction skill.** Its comparison step (write the model's default on the same material, then describe the differences), sample anonymisation, a generic-phrase gate, a second specificity pass and sample minimums are adapted in the contrast-extraction subskill and `scripts/profile_lint.py`. The file supplied names this method SICO. That name comes from Lu, Liu, He, Ong, Wang and Tang, "Large Language Models can be Guided to Evade AI-Generated Text Detection" (arXiv:2305.10847, first posted May 2023, published in Transactions on Machine Learning Research, 2024), where SICO stands for Substitution-based In-Context example Optimization. That paper's aim is getting model text past AI-text detectors. That aim is not used here, only the idea of learning from contrast. The original skill's author and licence were not stated in the copy supplied.
- **writing-style-extractor** by shannhk, crediting Machina (@exm7777). Its six-layer analysis is mapped to the nine calibration areas in `references/06`, section 8. The README states MIT, but no licence file was in the copy supplied, so check upstream before redistributing.
- **book-knowledge-extraction** (the maintainer's own skill). Evidence tiers, coverage records, clone and rebuild tests, and the unknowns register.

## Originality
