# Scene state ledger

Silent working sheet. Fill before drafting or continuing a scene. Never shown to the user unless they ask. Leave a field blank or write "unknown" rather than inventing.

- Character:
- Trying to accomplish right now:
- Occupying their attention:

- Expects to happen:

- Location and geography:
- Who is where, doing what:
- Objects carried:
- Equipment: working / damaged / missing:
- Physical injuries and fatigue:
- Timeline position:
- What the environment permits and prevents:

- Confirmed facts (reliable evidence in the story):
- Character knowledge (legitimately learned):
- Character beliefs (may be wrong):
- Character fears (worried about, not necessarily believed):
- Unresolved conflicts and disagreements:

- Wants:
- Obstacles:
- Information available:
- Actions or decisions possible:
- What changes by the end of the scene:
